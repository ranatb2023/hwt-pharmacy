## Forms we should ask for

Any form the staff fill in by hand is a specification we have not been given yet. Worth asking
for:

- [ ] The pharmacy's counter receipt, as currently printed
- [ ] The day-end / cash handover sheet, if one exists on paper
- [ ] The department indent or requisition slip used by the lab and the wards
- [ ] The welfare card itself, if one is already issued
- [ ] The udhaar khata register page

Getting the paper first is what made the dialysis module correct: the form told us the demand
is **per patient per shift**, which our earlier plan had wrong.
