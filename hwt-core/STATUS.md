## Dependency order

Phases must be started in this order. The arrows are hard dependencies, not preferences.

```
00 ──► 01 ──┬──► 02 ──┐
            │         ├──► 05 ──┐
            └──► 03 ──┤         ├──► 06 ──► 07
                      │         │
                      └──► 04 ──┘
                                └──────────────► 08
```

- **01 before 02** — a department invoice prices from the catalogue, and "company-wise sale"
  in 06 needs `manufacturer` to be a managed list.
- **03 before 02's invoicing** — a department is a customer type, so the customer record has
  to exist first. *(02 and 03 can run in parallel if 03's customer table lands first.)*
- **03 + 04 before 05** — dialysis needs entitlements and a ledger to post its demands to.
- **02, 03, 04, 05 before 06** — every report groups by something one of them introduced.
- **04 before 07** — the drawer must not open for credit sales, which requires credit to be a
  real payment method.
- **08 last** — and blocked on Q11 (is there any internet-connected machine?).
